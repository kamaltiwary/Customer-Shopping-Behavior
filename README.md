# 📊 Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis & Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project involves a comprehensive end-to-end data analytics pipeline designed to analyze **customer shopping behavior and transactional patterns** using a dataset of **3,900 purchases across 18 multi-dimensional behavioral columns**. 

The lifecycle spans automated data processing in **Python**, relational database warehousing in **PostgreSQL**, advanced analytical reporting using **SQL queries**, and executive-level visualization via an interactive **Power BI Dashboard**. The final platform serves as a centralized solution to optimize marketing spend, track customer loyalty, evaluate product performance, and drive subscription growth.

### 🎯 Key Project Objectives:
- **Centralize Behavioral Data:** Engineer a robust pipeline to clean, process, and migrate flat-file transactional logs into an enterprise-ready relational database structure.
- **Expose Purchasing Trends:** Analyze critical key indicators including demographic spending, seasonal demand shifts, and category-wise revenue distribution.
- **Evaluate Subscription & Loyalty Models:** Monitor the financial impact of premium subscribers versus non-subscribed users to design high-yield retention programs.
- **Optimize Shipping & Operational Choices:** Review the performance of distinct fulfillment channels (Express vs. Standard) to minimize overhead and enhance delivery satisfaction.
- **Deliver Actionable Business Insights:** Empower cross-functional stakeholders with an interactive dashboard, bridging raw analytical queries with high-impact strategic execution.

### 1️⃣ Data Preparation & Automation (Python)
Data engineering was executed inside a Jupyter Notebook environment using **Pandas** for transformation and **SQLAlchemy** with a `psycopg2` driver for database migration. 

- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Handled missing data points (including 37 missing values in the `Review Rating` column) and verified column data types via `.info()` and summary profiles via `.describe()`.
- **Database Streaming Automation:** Established a secure connection engine to stream the local data frame straight into a target PostgreSQL data warehouse using an optimized relational mapping profile.

## 🏆 Resolving Core Business Bottlenecks

### 1️⃣ Optimizing Subscription Models & Premium Conversions

* **The Problem:** The business lacked visibility into whether its subscription program was driving higher spending patterns or simply attracting existing low-value buyers.
* **The Dashboard Solution:** Cross-referenced historical purchase counts against premium tracking. The data revealed that frequent buyers with more than 5 previous orders showed specific subscription drop-off zones (2,518 non-subscribers vs. 958 premium subscribers).
* **Business Impact:** Allowed marketing teams to design custom promo-tier offers targeting the 2,518 highly frequent non-subscribers, unlocking massive upside in recurring revenue.

### 2️⃣ Category Performance & Inventory Allocation

* **The Problem:** Broad inventory planning created overstock situations in slow-moving categories while high-velocity items ran out of stock.
* **The Dashboard Solution:** Built explicit categorical tracking to isolate sales volumes across distinct segments (Clothing, Accessories, Footwear, and Outerwear) and mapped them directly against consumer age demographics.
* **Business Impact:** Revealed that the "Young Adult" demographic contributed the maximum revenue share ($62,143), leading to focused stock optimization and inventory adjustments tailored specifically to young adult product preferences.

### 3️⃣ Operational Shipping Adjustments

* **The Problem:** Shipping and logistics overheads were volatile, with a lack of data on whether express delivery models justified their higher operational costs.
* **The Dashboard Solution:** Quantified the exact mean purchase amounts across 2-Day Shipping, Express, Next Day Air, and Standard options.
* **Business Impact:** Allowed supply chain managers to see exactly where premium shipping options directly supported high-margin sales, leading to renegotiated logistics contracts and better delivery channel balancing.
